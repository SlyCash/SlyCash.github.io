Built-In Bike Lock | Arduino/C | Software Developer

My team designed and manufactured a built-in bike lock, where users don't have to bring or remember anything but their bike and a barcode (ID or create your own). The scanner scans a barcode ID to start the mechanical locking and unlocking mechanisms. I led the software development portion, while my team developed a 1/5 size, fully functional model, and we then completed the full size prototype. The scaled down model is shown for simplicity and visualization of components.
