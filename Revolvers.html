Revolvers | Unity | Current Project | Humanoid Animation

Now entering the early development of a co-op shooter, Revolvers has been planned out with two main pillars: Dual-Wield, and Weapon Flurishing. Players complete gun tricks, spins, and throws to improve aspects of their next shot, creating a motivation behind showing off. As a result, this project envolves a heavy load of humanoid animation, which I have been developing through the use of industry motion capture.
